FIRST RESEARCH BRIEF / FINTECH & DIGITAL STRATEGY

What should a cross-border money tool solve for international students?

Explore one customer segment in Milan first. Use a small set of Hong Kong interviews as a comparison only where it helps the decision.

8–12exploratory interviews
4–6comparable products
10–12slides in the final deck
Read the research brief

The decision: which user problem deserves a small product experiment? Compare transfer cost, clarity, convenience and trust without assuming the answer.

Scope: aim for 6–8 adult student interviews in Milan and 2–4 in Hong Kong. This is an exploratory convenience sample, not a representative survey. If access is limited, narrow to one city.

Method: ask about a recent real experience, compare public product information under the same transaction scenario, and separate observations from interpretations.

Output: an evidence table, anonymised themes, a concise deck, an alternative recommendation and a low-cost next experiment. Make every important number traceable.

Boundary: no bank credentials or account statements. Ask permission to take notes, minimise personal data and agree how long to keep them. This is student research, not financial advice or a client engagement.

MAKING IT WORK

Agree the working habits early.

Do we need to become a large club first?

Finish four consistent weeks and save the outputs before deciding to recruit. For internship applications, explaining your analysis and revisions is more useful than an empty title.

How do we prevent one partner doing everything?

Rotate interviewer, candidate and lead-writer roles each week. One partner starts the analysis; the other checks key calculations and sources. Credit actual contributions in the final deck.

Can we practise in Chinese before an English interview?

Use the familiar language to get the logic right, then present a two-minute conclusion in English. In the second half of the programme, run mocks in the language required by the role; practise reasoning, not only translation.

When should applications start?

As soon as a suitable role opens. Verify eligibility and deadlines in week one, then add verified project outcomes when they exist. There is no reason to wait for week eight.

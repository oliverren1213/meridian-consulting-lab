ABOUT US

Two perspectives.
One working habit.

Meridian is a proposed student learning initiative connecting the HEC–Bocconi programme and HKU. The aim is simple: learn consulting systematically, create useful independent research, and prepare for internships.

Commercial strategy, data and fintech meet an interest in politics, philosophy and economics. Both partners practise the full toolkit. Roles rotate every week.

Start with two people and one project. Revisit the format after week four. Expand only if the work is useful and the weekly commitment is sustainable.

FIRST RESEARCH BRIEF / FINTECH & DIGITAL STRATEGY

What should a cross-border money tool solve for international students?

Explore one customer segment in Milan first. Use a small set of Hong Kong interviews as a comparison only where it helps the decision.

8–12exploratory interviews
4–6comparable products
10–12slides in the final deck
Read the research brief

The decision: which user problem deserves a small product experiment? Compare transfer cost, clarity, convenience and trust without assuming the answer.

Scope: aim for 6–8 adult student interviews in Milan and 2–4 in Hong Kong. This is an exploratory convenience sample, not a representative survey. If access is limited, narrow to one city.

Method: ask about a recent real experience, compare public product information under the same transaction scenario, and separate observations from interpretations.

Output: an evidence table, anonymised themes, a concise deck, an alternative recommendation and a low-cost next experiment. Make every important number traceable.

Boundary: no bank credentials or account statements. Ask permission to take notes, minimise personal data and agree how long to keep them. This is student research, not financial advice or a client engagement.

03 / FROM PRACTICE TO OPPORTUNITY

Don’t wait until week eight.

Download the study kit
01

Know your target.

From week one, shortlist roles by actual work: consulting, boutique advisory, strategy or business development. Check year eligibility, language, location and deadlines.

OUTPUT / A SHORT, VERIFIED ROLE LIST
02

Ask better questions.

From week three, speak with alumni or practitioners about the work and recruitment. Ask a willing peer for a case review. Personal connections are not institutional partnerships.

OUTPUT / ONE USEFUL CONVERSATION
03

Apply with evidence.

Apply whenever a suitable opening is live. Build specific stories about your analysis, contribution and revisions. Describe the lab honestly as independent study and research.

OUTPUT / A TAILORED APPLICATION

Practice and a thoughtful project improve your preparation. They do not guarantee an internship or replace a firm’s eligibility requirements.

# Meridian Consulting Lab

An independent student learning initiative: eight weeks of consulting practice, exploratory fintech research and internship preparation.

Static English and Chinese website. No build step or account required. HKU, HEC Paris and Bocconi perspectives connect weekly practice to research and internship preparation. No university or student-club endorsement is implied.

Includes a weekly time planner, meeting-time converter, case timer, research toolkit and career playbook. Notes and weekly completion progress stay in the current browser; they are never uploaded or synced between users or devices.

The architectural hero image is an original AI-generated visual. Bundled Poppins fonts use the SIL Open Font License in assets/Poppins-LICENSE.txt.
